Privacy Policy - Fact Flip Hindi

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. About This Application

Fact Flip Hindi is an educational video content channel. Its automation application helps the channel owner create, manage and publish videos through authorized Google and YouTube APIs.

2. Information Accessed

With the account owner's permission, the application may access YouTube account information and permissions required to upload and manage videos.

3. How Information Is Used

Authorized information is used to operate the channel's video publishing workflow. It is not sold to advertisers.

4. Data Storage and Security

Authentication credentials and tokens are maintained in restricted application settings. They are not intentionally published on public websites or repositories.

5. Third-Party Services

This application uses Google and YouTube APIs. Google services are governed by the Google Privacy Policy .

6. Revoking Access

Users may revoke the application's access through their Google Account security settings.

7. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact the channel owner through the official Fact Flip Hindi channel contact details.
